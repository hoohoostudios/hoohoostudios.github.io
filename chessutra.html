Chessutra

A book of chess. Seven books, forty-nine chapters, and a new ruleset on every page.

The rules drift. The king still falls.

Coming soon to iPhone Book I is free

How it reads

Chessutra is laid out like an old book. Each chapter is one game of chess with one rule changed: knights that leap twice, a queen limited to three squares, a board whose edges wrap around, pieces that marry. Read the page, play the game, turn the page.

Book I keeps close to the chess you know. Each book after it drifts further. One thing never changes: you lose when your king is checkmated or captured, in every chapter.

Every chapter is played against a computer opponent that knows that chapter's rules. It all works offline, on the train or on a plane.
