From Book III

Your pieces can marry.

Do I have to be good at chess?

If you know how the pieces move, you can play.

Book I starts close to the game you know, and each book drifts a little further. Three levels let you pick your opponent, and medals mark every chapter you master.

Who do I play?

A computer that knows every rule.

Each chapter's opponent plays by that chapter's rules. No account, no ads, no connection needed. Play on the train, on a plane, at two in the morning.
