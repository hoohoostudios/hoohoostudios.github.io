Descriptions

Pick the length that fits.

One line

Chessutra is a book of chess: forty-nine chapters, each one a new set of rules, and the king still falls.

Short

Chessutra is a chess variant collection for iPhone, laid out like an old book. Seven books hold forty-nine chapters, and each chapter changes one rule of chess: pieces that marry, a board whose edges wrap around, captured pieces you can spend. The loss condition never changes. Your king falls, you lose.

Long

Chessutra takes the structure of the Kama Sutra, seven books of many chapters, and fills it with chess. Each chapter is one complete game with one rule changed. Book I stays close to the classical game: a king that wins by holding the centre, pawns that march backwards. Each book after it drifts further. Book II gives pieces new powers. Book V brings in the cannons, horses and elephants of the Elephant Game. Book VI is about exchange, with captured pieces going into a purse to be spent. Book VII bends the board itself until its edges meet.

Every chapter is played against an on-device opponent that knows its rules, and the whole game works offline. Book I is free. A single purchase unlocks the rest, including every chapter added later. Chessutra is made by Hoo Hoo Studios, the independent studio behind Grid Nite and Smooj.

Features

  • Forty-nine chess variants in seven themed books, one rule change per chapter
  • Chapters drawn from Western chess, the Elephant Game and invented rules: marriages, purses, ransoms, wrapping boards
  • A computer opponent for every chapter that understands that chapter's rules
  • Restrained, book-like design with a night mode, music and wooden-board sound
  • Works fully offline, with no ads and no tracking
