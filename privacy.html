Privacy Policy

Our apps don't collect anything. No accounts, no servers of ours, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. This page is mostly a list of things we don't do — plus an honest account of the few places your information does exist.

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

1) The Short Version

Hoo Hoo Studios ("Hoo Hoo," "we," "our," or "us") makes small iOS apps — currently Grid Nite and Smooj — and operates the website at hoohoo.studio.

  • Our apps collect no personal information. There is no sign-up, no account, and no server of ours for anything to be sent to.
  • We use no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs in our apps.
  • This website sets no cookies and makes no third-party requests.
  • We have never sold or shared personal information, and we never will. There is nothing to sell.
  • The only information we receive directly from you is what you choose to put in an email to us.

The rest of this page explains each of those, and is specific about the two places information does travel: Apple, and your own email. If you'd rather just ask a person, write to support@hoohoo.studio.

2) Our Apps

This applies to Grid Nite, Smooj, and any future app unless we say otherwise on this page before that app ships.

Everything an app stores — your puzzle progress, your settings, the moments you save — lives in that app's own storage on your device. It is not transmitted to us. We have no way to read it, no copy of it, and no ability to recover it for you if you delete it. Removing the app removes the data with it.

Specifically, our apps contain no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, attribution or install-tracking SDK, third-party crash reporter, or social login. They do not request your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or advertising identifier (IDFA), and they never present a tracking-permission prompt, because there is nothing we would do with the answer. They do not use Game Center or any other account system.

Smooj and iMessage. Smooj works inside iMessage, so what you send travels through Apple's iMessage to the person you send it to — the same as any other message you send. Apple carries and encrypts it end to end; it does not pass through any server of ours, and we never see its contents. Apple's handling of iMessage is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

3) This Website

This site is a handful of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from anyone else: the fonts, images, and stylesheet are all served from this domain. There are no tracking pixels, no CDN, no embedded video, and no CAPTCHA. Nothing on this site profiles you or follows you elsewhere.

The support form is not really a form. It is checked entirely in your browser and then opens a pre-filled draft in your own email app. Nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing reaches us until you decide to press send — at which point it arrives as ordinary email, covered by the next section.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it necessarily receives your IP address and browser user-agent in order to send you the page, and GitHub may retain those in standard server logs under its own privacy statement. We have no access to those logs and do not use them for anything.

4) When You Email Us

If you write to support@hoohoo.studio, we receive your email address, whatever you write, any attachments you include, and the routing information your mail provider attaches — the same as any email you send to anyone.

We use it to answer you, and for nothing else. We will not add you to a mailing list, and we will not pass your message to anyone else. We keep support threads only as long as they are useful for handling your request and for context if you write again, and we will delete a thread sooner if you ask.

5) What Apple Sees

Our apps are distributed through the App Store, so Apple sits between us and you, and Apple's own terms govern that relationship. Two things flow back to us from Apple, both aggregated and neither identifying you:

  • App Store reports. Counts of downloads and territories. Both apps are free with no in-app purchases, so there is no payment or billing information involved at any point.
  • App Analytics and crash reports. If you have turned on Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements > Share With App Developers, Apple may share aggregated usage statistics and crash logs with us. This is Apple's system, not ours, and it is off unless you opted in. You can turn it off at any time in that same screen, and we would never know the difference.

Apple's own collection is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

6) Children's Privacy

Our apps are made for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, there is no mechanism by which we could knowingly collect it from a child. If a child emails us, that message is treated like any other support email under section 4, and we will delete it on request from the child or their parent or guardian.

7) Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of it.

In practice these rights are easy for us to honour, because the only personal information we ever hold is email you have sent us. Write to support@hoohoo.studio and we will tell you exactly what we have — almost always just your previous emails — and delete it on request. There is no account to log into, no fee, and no need to prove your identity beyond writing from the address in question.

To state it plainly for California readers: we do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We never have.

8) Changes to This Policy

If we ever add something that changes what is described here — a server, an account system, an analytics tool, anything that collects data — we will update this page and the Effective Date above before it ships, not after.

For the record, earlier versions of this policy described advertising through Google AdMob in Grid Nite and a Google reCAPTCHA on the support form. Both have been removed, along with the web fonts and analytics the site once loaded. This policy describes what our apps and site actually do today.
